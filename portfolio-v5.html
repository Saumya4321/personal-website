About

GitHub LinkedIn saumya@gatech.edu

I am an MS student at Georgia Tech in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department. At Tech, I am affiliated with the Madabhushi Lab, advised by Anant Madabhushi, where I work on computational pathology and medical imaging AI.

Prior to grad school, I worked as a Project Associate at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bangalore under Haresh Dagale, where I developed real-time control systems for 3D printing infrastructure. Alongside this, I independently pursued research in medical imaging, and developed a brain tumor MRI segmentation model using U-Net and experimented with variance as an uncertainty estimate for MC Dropout uncertainty estimation, which is published on arXiv.

I graduated from M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology in 2023 with a BE in Electronics and Communication Engineering (CGPA 8.72).

Check out my GitHub for more details. Reach me at Email / LinkedIn.
